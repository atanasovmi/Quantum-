# Quantum Computing with Qiskit

A collection of Jupyter notebooks from the Quantum Disruption Class at FHNW Business School, demonstrating practical quantum computing applications using Qiskit and QASM (Quantum Assembly Language). These notebooks cover fundamental and advanced concepts in quantum programming, from circuit simulation to quantum machine learning.

## About This Repository

This repository contains coursework and projects developed as part of the Quantum Disruption Class at the University of Applied Sciences and Arts Northwestern Switzerland (FHNW) Business School. The majority of the notebooks were provided by our lecturers and instructors at FHNW Life Sciences in Muttenz and have been worked on collaboratively by our class.

## Notebooks

### Quantum Machine Learning (QML)
Explores quantum machine learning algorithms and their implementations, including quantum classifiers, variational quantum algorithms, and hybrid quantum-classical approaches.

### Quantum Image Encoding
Demonstrates techniques for encoding and processing classical images in quantum circuits, including FRQI (Flexible Representation of Quantum Images) and NEQR (Novel Enhanced Quantum Representation).

### Quantum Circuit Simulation
Covers quantum circuit simulation techniques, state vector analysis, and visualization of quantum operations using Qiskit's simulator backends.

### Quantum Optimization
Implements quantum optimization algorithms such as QAOA (Quantum Approximate Optimization Algorithm) and VQE (Variational Quantum Eigensolver) for solving combinatorial and optimization problems.

## Prerequisites

- Google Colab account (recommended) or Jupyter Notebook
